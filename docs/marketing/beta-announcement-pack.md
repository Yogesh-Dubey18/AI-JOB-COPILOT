## 💼 LinkedIn Launch Post

**Headline:** I just launched the public beta for **AI Job Copilot** — a open-source career cockpit for developers! 🚀

Finding a developer job is a numbers game, but tracking everything manually is exhausting. I built AI Job Copilot to give builders a personal job-search cockpit.

**What it does:**
- **Resume ATS Analyzer:** Analyzes resume keyword density and formatting.
- **Outreach Kit:** Tailors personalized cover letters and recruiter messages.
- **Interview Prep:**STAR method pre-interview check lists.
- **CRM Kanban Tracker:** Manages applications and recruiter contacts.
- **Privacy First:** Redacts personal details before sending data to AI.
