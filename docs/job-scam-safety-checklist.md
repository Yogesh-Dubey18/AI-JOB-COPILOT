# Job Scam Safety Checklist

Use before applying to unknown companies or responding to recruiters.

## Red Flags

- Payment, deposit, registration fee, training fee, or equipment fee.
- Salary far above market for the experience level.
- Recruiter uses free email for a company that should have a domain.
- No company website or public presence.
- Vague job description with no real responsibilities.
- Pressure to join immediately.
- Requests for sensitive documents before interview.
- Interview only through suspicious chat channels.

## Verification Steps

- Check company website.
- Search company name plus role title.
- Verify email domain.
- Check LinkedIn company page.
- Compare job details with official career page.
- Ask for written job details if unclear.

## Action

- Low risk: apply normally.
- Medium risk: verify before sharing more data.
- High risk: avoid applying and record the risk.
