# Referral Request Log

Referrals work best when the request is specific and low-pressure.

## Referral Request Checklist

- Confirm the role is active.
- Share the official job link.
- Share resume and project links.
- State that referral is optional.
- Do not pressure the person.
- Thank them regardless of outcome.

## Follow-Up Timing

- Follow up once after 4 to 5 business days.
- Do not follow up repeatedly if there is no response.
