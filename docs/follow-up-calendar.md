## Schedule

- Application: 5 to 7 business days.
- Recruiter: 3 to 5 business days.
- Referral: 4 to 5 business days.
- Interview: within 24 hours.
- Assignment: 2 to 3 business days after submission.

## Stop Rule

Stop after two unanswered follow-ups unless the recruiter explicitly asks you to check later.
