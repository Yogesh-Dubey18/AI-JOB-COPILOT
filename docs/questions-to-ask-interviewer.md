# Questions To Ask Interviewer

Use 2 to 4 questions depending on time.

## Role

- What would success look like for this role in the first 3 months?
- What kind of tasks would a fresher or junior developer handle first?
- Which technical skills are most important for this team?

## Team

- How is code reviewed in the team?
- How do developers collaborate with product or design?
- What does the onboarding process look like?

## Product

- What are the biggest product or engineering priorities right now?
- Are there any upcoming features where this role would contribute?

## Growth

- How does the team support learning and feedback?
- What should I prepare before joining if selected?
