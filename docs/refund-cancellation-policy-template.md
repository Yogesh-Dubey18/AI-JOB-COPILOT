# Refund And Cancellation Policy Template

Template status: requires legal, tax, and payment-provider review before charging users.

## Cancellation

Users should be able to cancel a paid plan from billing settings or by contacting support. Access may continue until the end of the current billing period unless local law or the final policy says otherwise.

## Refunds

Possible policy options to review:

- Refunds within a short window after first purchase.
- No refunds after substantial AI usage.
- Exceptions for duplicate charges, billing errors, or legally required refunds.

## Non-Refundable Usage

AI credits, resume exports, application kits, and mock interview sessions may involve provider costs. The final policy should clearly explain how usage affects refunds.

## Required Before Launch

- Stripe billing flow configured.
- Webhook handling verified.
- Tax/invoice settings reviewed.
- Support process for refund requests defined.
- Final wording reviewed by a professional.
