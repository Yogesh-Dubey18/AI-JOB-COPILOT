# LinkedIn Personal Branding System

Use LinkedIn to show learning, product thinking, and engineering discipline without overclaiming.

## Profile Positioning

- Headline: Full-stack developer focused on AI-powered job-search tools and production-ready SaaS architecture.
- About: Explain your skills, project, learning path, and openness to fresher/entry-level roles.
- Featured: Add AI Job Copilot repo, demo video if available, and case study.

## Weekly Content Ideas

- What you improved in AI Job Copilot this week.
- A technical lesson from debugging build/test issues.
- A short breakdown of one feature architecture.
- A before/after UI or documentation improvement.
- A learning note from interviews or job search feedback.

## Post Quality Checklist

- Is it honest?
- Does it teach something specific?
- Does it avoid fake metrics?
- Does it point to real work?
- Would you be comfortable explaining it in an interview?

## Outreach Rule

Use LinkedIn for respectful human outreach. Do not spam recruiters or send AI-generated messages without review.
