## Monthly Reflection

- What did I ship?
- What did I learn?
- What feedback did I receive?
- What should I stop doing?
- What should I do next week?

## Evidence Types

- Commit hash.
- Test/build result.
- Screenshot or short demo.
- Interview feedback.
- Recruiter response.
- Measurable learning output, such as solved problems or completed project improvements.
