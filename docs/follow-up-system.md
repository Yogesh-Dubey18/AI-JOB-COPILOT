# Follow-Up System

Follow-ups should be respectful, brief, and tied to a real application. Do not spam recruiters.

## Timing

- Application follow-up: 5 to 7 business days after applying.
- Recruiter message follow-up: 3 to 5 business days after the first message.
- Interview follow-up: within 24 hours after the interview.
- Assignment follow-up: 2 to 3 business days after submission.

## Follow-Up Template

Hi [Name], I hope you are doing well. I wanted to follow up on my application for [Role] at [Company], submitted on [Date]. I am still very interested in the opportunity and believe my React/Node.js project experience aligns with the role. Please let me know if there is any update or anything else I can share.

## Interview Thank You

Hi [Name], thank you for speaking with me today about the [Role] opportunity. I enjoyed discussing [specific topic]. The conversation made me more interested in the role, especially because [reason]. I look forward to the next steps.

## Tracker Fields

- Contact name.
- Channel.
- First message date.
- Follow-up date.
- Response.
- Next action.
- Stop condition.

## Stop Conditions

- The recruiter says the role is closed.
- You have already followed up twice with no response.
- The job post has expired.
- The company shows scam or trust concerns.
