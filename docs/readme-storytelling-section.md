## AI Job Copilot

AI Job Copilot is a full-stack SaaS demo that helps job seekers manage the full job-search workflow from resume upload to interview preparation.

The product is intentionally not a job board and not an auto-apply tool. It keeps candidates in control by generating reviewable drafts, highlighting role fit, tracking applications, and creating skill/interview plans.

Engineering highlights:

- Next.js App Router frontend.
- Express TypeScript backend.
- MongoDB/Mongoose-ready persistence.
- JWT auth and role-protected admin.
- AI provider abstraction with mock fallback.
- Resume analysis, job matching, tailored resumes, application kits, interviews, analytics, notifications, feedback, privacy, billing-ready architecture, PWA, PDF exports, and extension foundation.
- Build/test/security/docs/git-safety verification loop.
