# Offer Negotiation Guide

This guide is a template, not legal or financial advice.

## Before Negotiating

- Understand role responsibilities.
- Compare market range.
- Review location and work mode.
- Review learning opportunity.
- Review benefits and joining terms.

## Negotiation Principles

- Be respectful.
- Use data, not pressure.
- Explain value clearly.
- Be flexible as a fresher.
- Avoid ultimatums.

## Template

Thank you for the offer. I am excited about the opportunity to join [Company] as [Role]. Based on the responsibilities, market range, and my project experience in full-stack development, would it be possible to review the compensation to [range]? I remain very interested in the role and would be happy to discuss.
