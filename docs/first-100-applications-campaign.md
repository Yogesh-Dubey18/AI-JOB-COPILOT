# First 100 Applications Campaign

## Goal

Submit 100 high-quality, manual, tracked applications without spamming or applying to unsafe roles.

## Rules

- Apply manually through official or trusted links.
- Tailor resume for strong-fit roles.
- Do not fake experience.
- Do not apply to jobs that require payment.
- Track every application.
- Follow up respectfully.

## Targets

- 100 applications.
- 50 recruiter messages.
- 25 referral requests.
- 10 mock interviews.
- Weekly review after every 25 applications.

## Success Metrics

- Response rate.
- Interview rate.
- Best job source.
- Best resume version.
- Most common rejection reason.
