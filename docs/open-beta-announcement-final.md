## 📢 LinkedIn Launch Post

```text
🚀 Excited to share that I've just launched the Open Beta for AI Job Copilot!

AI Job Copilot is a dedicated career cockpit and CRM tracker built specifically for job-seeking developers. It helps you organize your search, evaluate resume keyword gaps, and prepare for interviews.

👉 Live Link: https://ai-job-copilot-frontend.vercel.app

What's inside the platform:
- 📊 ATS Score Checker: Estimate how well your resume matches target keywords.
- 📁 Recruiter CRM & Kanban Board: Track application statuses, dates, and follow-ups.
- 📧 Tailored Application Kits: Generate initial drafts for cover letters and recruiter outreach messages.
- 💬 AI Mentor Chat: Work through role-specific coding and behavior questions.

⚠️ Honest Beta Notes & Limitations:
- Real-First AI: Every AI-tailored output requires your explicit review and refinement before submission. The platform does not auto-apply for you.
- Sandbox Integrations: External services (like Google OAuth, Stripe Billing, and direct job-feed APIs) are currently running on sandbox fallback mode until production keys are configured. Account registration is via email/password.
- No Guarantees: This is a workflow organization tool. It does not guarantee interviews, job placements, or offers.

Since it is in beta, your feedback is extremely valuable. If you give it a try, please submit any feedback using the in-app "/feedback" page or drop a comment below!
```

## 💡 Short Product Pitch

> **AI Job Copilot** is a privacy-conscious, developer-first career cockpit and CRM tracker. Instead of relying on uncontrolled automation, it provides job seekers with the organization, resume keyword insights, and preparation tools needed to navigate the search process manually. By prioritizing "review-first AI," the platform ensures candidates remain in full control of every application they submit.
