# LinkedIn Storytelling Posts

## Post 1: Product Build

I built AI Job Copilot, a full-stack AI SaaS project for job seekers.

The idea is simple: upload your resume once, then use it to analyze ATS readiness, find matching jobs, tailor application materials, track applications, prepare for interviews, and improve from feedback.

Important boundary: it does not auto-apply and it does not auto-send messages. AI outputs are user-reviewed drafts.

Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Express, MongoDB/Mongoose-ready architecture, JWT auth, mock/provider-ready AI, tests, deployment docs, admin, analytics, PWA, PDF exports, and extension foundation.

## Post 2: Engineering Lessons

What I learned building AI Job Copilot:

- AI products need guardrails, not just prompts.
- Mock fallback makes demos and tests reliable.
- SaaS architecture needs usage tracking, billing readiness, admin operations, audit logs, and privacy flows.
- Job-search tools must avoid spam and fake experience.
- Documentation matters when a project is meant to be maintained.
