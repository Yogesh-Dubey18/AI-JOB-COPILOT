# Joining Documents Checklist

Common documents may include:

- Resume.
- Government ID.
- Education certificates.
- Mark sheets.
- Passport-size photos.
- Bank details.
- PAN/tax details if applicable.
- Address proof.
- Offer acceptance email.
- Previous internship/employment documents if any.

## Safety

Share documents only through official company channels. Avoid sending sensitive documents to unverified contacts.
