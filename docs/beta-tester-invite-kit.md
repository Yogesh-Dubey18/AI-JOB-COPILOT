# Beta Tester Invite Kit

This invite kit contains templates for recruiting and onboarding the first wave of beta testers for the **AI Job Copilot** platform.

---

## 📧 Email Outreach Templates

### Template 1: Warm Contact (Friends, Colleagues, Peer Developers)
**Subject:** Help me test AI Job Copilot? (Beta Launch)

Hi [Name],

I am launching the public beta for **AI Job Copilot**, a personal career cockpit built to assist job seekers with resume tailoring, ATS matching, interview preparation, and application tracking.

I would love to get your feedback as an early developer/builder. You can try the live app here:
👉 **Frontend:** https://ai-job-copilot-frontend.vercel.app

**What to test:**
1. Try uploading your resume at `/resume/upload`.
2. Inspect your resume score in the ATS analyzer.
3. Check the guided workflow at `/guided-workflow`.
4. Try generating tailored answers for behavioral or technical interview rounds.

If the backend takes a moment to respond, it is just Render waking up from a cold start. If you run into issues, there is a **"Continue in Demo Mode"** fallback button right on the login screen that lets you explore the dashboard with pre-loaded mock metrics.

Please log any bugs or feature feedback using the feedback page in the app, or reply directly to this email.

Thanks,  
[Your Name]
