# Product Storytelling Package

## One-Line Story

AI Job Copilot helps job seekers turn one resume into a guided job-search operating system: resume analysis, job matching, tailored application assets, tracking, interview prep, and feedback-driven improvement.

## Problem

Entry-level job seekers often manage resumes, job posts, outreach, applications, interview prep, and rejection learning across scattered tools. They need structure, not another job board.

## Product Angle

AI Job Copilot is a personal career assistant for job seekers. It is not an employer job-posting platform and it does not auto-apply. It keeps the user in control and turns AI outputs into reviewed drafts.

## Differentiators

- Resume-first workflow.
- AI mock fallback for local demos.
- Job matching with missing skills and trust signals.
- Application tracker and follow-up system.
- Interview coach and PDF exports.
- Privacy/export/delete foundations.
- SaaS, admin, billing-ready, audit, monitoring, and feedback-loop architecture.

## Honest Boundaries

- Live deployment is pending external platform setup.
- AI/email/billing/monitoring providers are mock/provider-ready until keys are configured.
- Legal and commercial materials are templates requiring professional review.
