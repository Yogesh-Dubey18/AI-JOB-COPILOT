# Pricing Validation Plan

## Goal

Validate whether Free, Pro, and Premium tiers match real job-seeker willingness to pay without overclaiming outcomes.

## Hypotheses

- Free users value resume analysis and basic tracking.
- Pro users pay for resume tailoring, application kits, and daily digest workflows.
- Premium users pay for mock interviews, portfolio generation, and deeper mentor guidance.

## Validation Steps

1. Interview 10 target users.
2. Run a no-payment pricing preference survey.
3. Track which features users try repeatedly.
4. Compare AI provider cost against credit limits.
5. Test upgrade prompts without charging users.
6. Configure Stripe only after policy review.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim guaranteed job outcomes.
- Do not pressure unemployed users with misleading scarcity.
- Keep cancellation and refund terms clear.
- Keep mock billing labelled as mock until Stripe is live.
