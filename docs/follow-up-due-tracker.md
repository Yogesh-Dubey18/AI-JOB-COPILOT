## Follow-Up Categories

- Application follow-up.
- Recruiter follow-up.
- Referral follow-up.
- Interview thank-you.
- Assignment submission follow-up.

## Stop Conditions

- Two unanswered follow-ups.
- Role closed.
- Rejection received.
- Scam or trust risk found.
- You are no longer interested in the role.
