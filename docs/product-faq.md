# Product FAQ

## Is AI Job Copilot a job board?

No. It is a personal AI career assistant for job seekers.

## Does it auto-apply?

No. Users apply manually through official job links.

## Does it auto-send recruiter messages?

No. Messages are generated as drafts for user review.

## Does it guarantee selection?

No. It helps prepare better materials and workflows, but it cannot guarantee interviews, offers, salary, or employment.

## Does it use real AI providers?

The architecture supports OpenAI and Gemini, but mock fallback works when keys are missing.

## Is billing live?

No. Billing is mock/Stripe-ready until real provider keys, webhooks, tax settings, refund policy, and support operations are configured.

## Is it production deployed?

Not unless real live URLs are provided and verified. The repo contains deployment runbooks and env checklists.
