# Professional Workplace Message Templates

## Task Clarification

Hi [Name], I want to confirm my understanding of [task]. The expected outcome is [outcome], and I should avoid [constraint]. Is that correct?

## Delay Update

Hi [Name], this task is taking longer than expected because [reason]. I have completed [done] and am working on [next]. I expect to update by [time].

## Feedback Request

Hi [Name], could you please share feedback on my approach for [task]? I want to make sure I am following the team's standards.

## Thank You

Thank you for the review and suggestions. I will update the changes and keep the feedback in mind for future tasks.
