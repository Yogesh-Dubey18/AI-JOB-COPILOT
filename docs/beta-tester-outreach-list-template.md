## 📈 Status Legend
- **Invited:** Invitation sent, waiting for registration.
- **Active:** Registered and has logged in.
- **Feedback Received:** Submitted feedback via form, email, or interview.
- **Declined:** Opted out of the beta testing.
- **Churned:** Registered but has not performed any activities for > 4 days.

---

## 💡 Best Practices for outreach
1. **Target Mix:** Have at least 60% active job seekers (Devs), 20% passive job seekers, and 20% recruiters or managers to test the Recruiter Portal views.
2. **Personalized Invites:** Use the email and DM templates from the [Beta Tester Invite Kit](beta-tester-invite-kit.md). Do not bulk-send; personalize the first sentence for each tester.
3. **Follow-up Cadence:** Follow up exactly 48 hours after the invite if they haven't registered, and 72 hours after registration if they haven't uploaded a resume.
