# Recruiter Message Templates

All messages are user-review templates. Personalize the role, company, project, and availability before sending.

## LinkedIn Recruiter Message

Hi [Name], I noticed the [Role] opening at [Company]. I am a fresher full-stack developer with hands-on React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, JavaScript, Java, and DSA practice. I recently built AI Job Copilot, a full-stack SaaS project with auth, resume analysis, job matching, application tracking, admin tools, and deployment-ready docs.  

I would be grateful if you could review my profile for this role. I can share my resume and project links.

## Email Message

My recent project, AI Job Copilot, demonstrates full-stack product thinking, AI fallback architecture, SaaS workflows, testing, and deployment readiness.

Regards,  
[Your Name]

## Short Follow-Up

Hi [Name], I wanted to follow up on my application for [Role] at [Company]. I remain interested because the role matches my React/Node.js project experience and learning goals. Please let me know if I can share any additional details.
