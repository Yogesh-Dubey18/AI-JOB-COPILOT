# Offer Acceptance Checklist

Before accepting:

- Offer letter received.
- Role title correct.
- Salary and benefits clear.
- Joining date clear.
- Work location clear.
- Probation period clear.
- Bond or agreement terms reviewed.
- Required documents listed.
- Contact person confirmed.

## Acceptance Template

Hi [Name], thank you for the offer for [Role] at [Company]. I am happy to accept the offer and look forward to joining the team. Please let me know the next steps and required documents.
