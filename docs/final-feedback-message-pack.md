# Final Feedback Message Pack

This file contains copy-pasteable communication templates for the beta program, ranging from initial outreach to follow-ups and issue resolution notifications.

---

## 📨 Cohort Onboarding Messages

### Template 1: LinkedIn / WhatsApp Warm Invite
> "Hi **{First Name}**! I hope you're doing well. I've launched the public beta for **AI Job Copilot** (https://ai-job-copilot-frontend.vercel.app) — a career cockpit I've been building for developers. It covers resume ATS parsing, customized cover letters, and has a built-in recruiter CRM and Kanban tracker.
> 
> Since you are currently looking for new roles, I'd value your feedback. Could you sign up and try uploading a resume? Let me know if you run into any layout bugs or issues. Thank you!"

### Template 2: Recruiter / Hiring Manager Invite
> "Hi **{First Name}**! I'm sharing a prototype of a developer-focused career cockpit called **AI Job Copilot** (https://ai-job-copilot-frontend.vercel.app). 
> 
> Along with job seeker tools, we have a Recruiter Portal (at `/recruiters`) to help companies find pre-verified developer profiles. I'd love your input on candidate privacy and what metrics you'd want in a recruiter dashboard. Let me know what you think!"

## ⏰ Tester Follow-up Nudges

### Template 3: Signed Up but No Resume Uploaded (Day 3 Nudge)
> "Hi **{First Name}**! Thanks for signing up for the AI Job Copilot beta. I noticed you haven't uploaded a resume to test out the ATS analyzer yet.
> 
> If you have 5 minutes, could you upload your resume PDF at https://ai-job-copilot-frontend.vercel.app/resume/upload and verify if the parser extracts your experience accurately? It really helps us test parsing heuristics. Thanks!"

### Template 4: General Weekly Nudge
> "Hey **{First Name}**! Just checking in on how your job search is going and if you've been using AI Job Copilot to track your applications. 
> 
> If you've encountered any issues or have ideas to improve the tracker board (`/applications`) or interview prep tools (`/interviews`), feel free to drop them here. Good luck with the applications!"

## 🛠️ Bug Triage & Fix Notifications

### Template 5: Bug Report Intake Acknowledgment
> "Hi **{First Name}**,
> 
> Thank you for reporting the issue regarding **{Description}**.
> 
> I have logged this on our feedback tracking board under ID **{Issue ID}** (https://github.com/Yogesh-Dubey18/AI-JOB-COPILOT/blob/main/docs/beta-feedback-triage-board.md). We are investigating the cause and will let you know once a patch is deployed to production.
> 
> Thank you for helping us polish the beta!"

### Template 6: Bug Fix Confirmation
> "Hi **{First Name}**,
> 
> Good news! We have successfully patched the issue you reported regarding **{Description}** (Issue **{Issue ID}**).
> 
> The fix is now live in production. Could you refresh your browser (https://ai-job-copilot-frontend.vercel.app) and confirm if it resolves the problem on your end?
> 
> Thanks again for your valuable feedback!"
