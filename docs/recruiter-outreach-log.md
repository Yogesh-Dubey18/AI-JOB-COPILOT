# Recruiter Outreach Log

Track recruiter outreach so messages stay respectful and non-spammy.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Message Rules

- Mention the role and company.
- Keep the first message short.
- Include one truthful project signal.
- Follow up once after 3 to 5 business days.
- Stop after two unanswered messages.

## Response Categories

- Positive reply.
- Asked for resume.
- Asked to apply online.
- Role closed.
- Not relevant.
- No response.
