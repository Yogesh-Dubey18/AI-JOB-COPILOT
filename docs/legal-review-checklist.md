# Legal Review Checklist

Template status: this checklist is not legal advice and must be reviewed by a qualified professional.

## Policies

- [ ] Terms of service.
- [ ] Privacy policy.
- [ ] Cookie or tracking notice if analytics are enabled.
- [ ] Refund and cancellation policy.
- [ ] Acceptable use policy.
- [ ] AI disclaimer.
- [ ] Support policy.

## Product Claims

- [ ] No guarantee of job outcomes.
- [ ] No claim of automatic applications.
- [ ] No false claims about partnerships with job boards, employers, or recruiters.
- [ ] No fake metrics, users, revenue, funding, or endorsements.
- [ ] Clear mock/demo/provider-ready labeling where relevant.

## Data Handling

- [ ] Resume data handling reviewed.
- [ ] AI provider data sharing reviewed.
- [ ] Data export and deletion wording reviewed.
- [ ] Retention periods approved.
- [ ] Security logs and backups addressed.

## Commercial

- [ ] Billing terms reviewed.
- [ ] Tax and invoice requirements reviewed.
- [ ] Cancellation and refund window reviewed.
- [ ] Support response commitments reviewed.
