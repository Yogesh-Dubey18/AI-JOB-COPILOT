# Job Search System

AI Job Copilot supports the product workflow, but the job search itself must stay user-reviewed and manual. This system turns the app into a daily operating rhythm for finding roles, tailoring materials, applying through official links, and learning from every result.

## Daily Flow

1. Open the dashboard and review profile completeness, ATS score, missing skills, and recommended jobs.
2. Pick a target role for the day, such as React Developer, MERN Stack Developer, Java Developer, or Frontend Intern.
3. Review 10 to 20 jobs from trusted sources and shortlist only roles that match skills, location, salary range, and experience level.
4. Run job match or scam checks before spending time on an application.
5. Tailor the resume and application kit only for roles worth applying to.
6. Apply manually through the official company page or trusted job board.
7. Add every application to the tracker with status, source, resume version, and follow-up date.
8. End the day by logging outcomes and updating the next action list.

## Weekly Targets

- Quality applications: 25 to 40.
- Recruiter messages: 20 to 30.
- Referral requests: 10 to 15.
- Resume variants tested: 2 to 4.
- Interview practice sessions: 3 to 5.
- Projects or skills improved: 1 focused improvement.

## Quality Rules

- Do not apply to jobs that ask for payment, registration fees, suspicious personal data, or off-platform payments.
- Do not add skills, experience, companies, internships, or project claims that are not true.
- Do not use the same generic resume for every role.
- Do not auto-send AI messages without reading and personalizing them.
- Prefer official company career pages when available.

## Review Cadence

- Daily: update application status and next follow-ups.
- Twice weekly: inspect response rate by resume version and source.
- Weekly: update top missing skills and interview prep focus.
- Monthly: archive low-quality sources and improve the portfolio/project story.
