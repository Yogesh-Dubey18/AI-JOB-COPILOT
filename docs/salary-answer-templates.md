# Salary Answer Templates

## Fresher Range Answer

I am open to a fair fresher-level package based on company standards, role responsibilities, location, and learning opportunity.

## If Asked For Expected Salary

Based on my skills and the role expectations, I am looking for a fair market-aligned fresher package. I am flexible and more focused on the right learning and growth opportunity.

## If Offer Is Low

Thank you for sharing the offer. I am grateful for the opportunity. Would it be possible to review the compensation based on the role responsibilities and current market range?
