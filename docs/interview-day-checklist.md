# Interview Day Checklist

## Before Interview

- Resume open.
- Job description open.
- Project links tested.
- Internet and audio checked.
- Quiet environment ready.
- Water nearby.
- Notebook ready.

## 30-Minute Review

- Tell me about yourself.
- AI Job Copilot explanation.
- Role-specific skills.
- One DSA problem.
- Questions to ask interviewer.

## During Interview

- Speak slowly.
- Think aloud for coding.
- Be honest when unsure.
- Connect answers to real project work.
- Ask clarifying questions.

## After Interview

- Send thank-you note.
- Log questions asked.
- Record weak topics.
- Schedule follow-up.
