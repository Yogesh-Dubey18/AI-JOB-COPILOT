# Final Project Pitch For Interviews

## 30 Seconds

AI Job Copilot is a full-stack AI-powered job-search SaaS assistant for job seekers. It helps users upload resumes, get ATS analysis, see matching jobs, tailor resumes, generate user-reviewed application messages, track applications, prepare for interviews, and review analytics.

## 2 Minutes

I built AI Job Copilot as a complete product workflow instead of a single feature. The frontend uses Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. The backend uses Node.js, Express, TypeScript, and MongoDB/Mongoose-ready models. It includes JWT auth, resume upload, job matching, AI fallback architecture, applications, interviews, analytics, admin, billing-ready structure, tests, and deployment docs.

The app intentionally does not auto-apply or auto-send messages. AI outputs are reviewable by the user.
