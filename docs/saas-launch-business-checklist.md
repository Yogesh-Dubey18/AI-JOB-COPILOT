# SaaS Launch Business Checklist

Template status: requires business, legal, tax, and payment-provider review before paid launch.

## Business Setup

- [ ] Business entity or owner details decided.
- [ ] Support email configured.
- [ ] Payment provider account configured.
- [ ] Tax/invoicing requirements reviewed.
- [ ] Pricing and refund policy approved.

## Product Setup

- [ ] Frontend deployed.
- [ ] Backend deployed.
- [ ] MongoDB Atlas configured.
- [ ] AI provider configured or mock mode clearly labelled.
- [ ] Email provider configured for real notifications.
- [ ] Monitoring configured.

## Legal And Trust

- [ ] Privacy policy reviewed.
- [ ] Terms reviewed.
- [ ] AI disclaimer reviewed.
- [ ] Refund/cancellation policy reviewed.
- [ ] Incident response owner assigned.

## Go-Live Decision

Launch only after smoke tests, legal review, support ownership, and provider dashboards are ready.
