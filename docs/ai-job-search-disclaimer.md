# AI Job Search Disclaimer

Template status: requires professional review before public commercial use.

AI Job Copilot provides career, resume, job-search, application, and interview preparation assistance. It is an advisory tool, not an employment agency, recruiter, employer, legal advisor, financial advisor, or immigration advisor.

## Important Limits

- AI outputs can be incomplete or incorrect.
- Users must review all resume, cover letter, recruiter message, salary answer, and interview content before use.
- The product must not be used to invent work experience, education, skills, projects, certifications, or achievements.
- The product does not auto-apply to jobs or auto-send messages.
- The product does not guarantee interviews, offers, salary increases, selection, or employment.

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for:

- Verifying job postings and employer legitimacy.
- Reviewing all generated content.
- Applying through official job links.
- Following each employer's application rules.
- Avoiding spam, misrepresentation, and unsafe outreach.

## Scam And Safety

Scam detection is a risk signal, not a guarantee. Users should independently verify companies, domains, payment requests, suspicious salaries, and recruiter identity.
