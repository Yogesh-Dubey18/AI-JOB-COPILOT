# Post-Interview Follow-Up System

## Within 24 Hours

Send a thank-you message.

## Template

Hi [Name], thank you for speaking with me about the [Role] opportunity. I enjoyed discussing [specific topic]. I remain interested in the role and would be happy to share any additional information if needed.
