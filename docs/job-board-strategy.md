# Job Board Strategy

Use job boards as discovery sources, then verify roles on official company pages when possible.

## Recommended Source Mix

- Company career pages for highest trust.
- LinkedIn for recruiter discovery and public roles.
- Well-known job boards for volume.
- College placement groups only when verified.
- Community job channels only after scam checks.
- Referral leads from alumni, seniors, and developers.

## Search Query Patterns

- `"React Developer fresher remote"`
- `"MERN Stack Developer junior"`
- `"Frontend Developer Intern React"`
- `"Node.js Developer entry level"`
- `"Java Developer fresher"`
- `"Full Stack Developer internship remote"`

## Source Quality Signals

- Company website exists and matches the job posting.
- Recruiter email uses company domain.
- Job description has specific responsibilities and skills.
- Salary range is realistic for the role and location.
- Interview process does not require payment.

## Low-Quality Signals

- Payment demand or registration fee.
- Gmail/Yahoo recruiter email for a large company.
- Unrealistic salary for no experience.
- Vague role description with no company details.
- Pressure to join immediately without interview.
