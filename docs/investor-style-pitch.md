# Investor-Style Pitch

Template for storytelling only. Do not claim revenue, traction, funding, users, partnerships, or market validation unless real evidence exists.

## Problem

Millions of early-career candidates struggle with fragmented job-search workflows, weak resumes, unclear role fit, poor tracking, and inconsistent interview preparation.

## Solution

AI Job Copilot turns the job search into a guided operating system: resume intelligence, job matching, application assets, tracking, interview coach, skill roadmap, and analytics.

## Why Now

AI can help candidates personalize preparation, but trust requires user review, privacy controls, and guardrails against fake experience or spam.

## Product Principles

- Job seeker first.
- Resume once, reuse intelligently.
- Never auto-apply.
- Never auto-send.
- Keep outputs reviewable.
- Keep claims honest.

## Business Model Hypothesis

Free, Pro, and Premium tiers based on AI credits, resume tailoring, application kits, mock interviews, portfolio tools, and mentor guidance. Pricing requires validation before commercialization.

## Current Status

Built and locally verified as a full-stack architecture/demo project. Production deployment and commercial launch require the documented manual gates.
