# Product Comparison Notes

These notes are for positioning, not for claiming superiority over existing companies.

## Not A Job Board

AI Job Copilot does not replace LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri, Internshala, or company career pages. It helps job seekers prepare and manage applications after they find opportunities.

## Not An Auto-Apply Tool

The app does not apply on the user's behalf and does not send recruiter messages automatically.

## Compared With Generic AI Chat

Generic AI chat can write drafts, but AI Job Copilot gives structure:

- Resume profile context.
- Job match scoring.
- Application tracker.
- Interview history.
- Analytics.
- Admin and feedback loop.
- Privacy/export/delete workflows.

## Compared With Spreadsheet Tracking

Spreadsheets can track applications, but they do not connect resume analysis, AI prep, follow-up reminders, job match signals, and interview readiness in one workflow.
