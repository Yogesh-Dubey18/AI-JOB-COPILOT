# Commercial Risk Register

Template status: business risk draft requiring professional review.

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI output is incorrect | User trust and career harm | User review requirement, disclaimers, feedback loop | Product |
| User invents experience | Ethical and trust risk | Guardrails and copy that rejects fake experience | Product |
| Resume data exposure | Privacy/security risk | Auth, user scoping, privacy docs, incident plan | Engineering |
| Billing dispute | Support and financial risk | Clear refund/cancellation policy | Business |
| Provider outage | Product availability risk | Mock fallback and status pages | Engineering |
| Job scam false negative | User safety risk | Risk signal language and manual verification guidance | Product |
| Legal pages not reviewed | Commercial/legal risk | Professional review before paid launch | Owner |
| Support backlog grows | Reputation risk | Support process and triage cadence | Operations |

## Review Cadence

- Review before every public launch.
- Review after incidents.
- Review after pricing or policy changes.
